01 / WHO WE ARE

Technical insight.
Practical maritime support.

Nautical Inspection & Survey is a Chattogram-based marine survey and cargo inspection service provider supporting cargo owners, traders, shipping interests and survey principals.

Our work focuses on documenting cargo quantity, cargo condition and handling activities at critical stages of marine operations. We combine field observations, relevant measurements and supporting records to deliver reports that are clear about findings and limitations.

02 / OUR EXPERTISE

Marine & Cargo Services

From initial vessel attendance to final cargo reconciliation, our services are defined by the requirements of each appointment.

All services are subject to agreed scope, safe access, available records and operational arrangements. Laboratory analysis or third-party specialist work is arranged only when expressly agreed.
OUR SERVICE PHILOSOPHY
“A useful survey does more than state a result. It explains what was examined, how it was measured and where the limitations lie.”

04 / REPORTING

Documentation that
stands up to review.

Survey findings are most valuable when their basis is clear.

Depending on the assignment, deliverables may include initial and final draft survey reports, cargo condition reports, photographic records, lighter-wise distribution statements, daily discharge updates, Statement of Facts and final reconciliation reports.
